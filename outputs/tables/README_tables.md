# Machine-readable result tables

The CSV files in this directory mirror the principal numerical results reported in the manuscript and Supplementary Materials.

- `table5_descriptive_statistics.csv`: complete-panel descriptive statistics.
- `table6_groupwise_comparison.csv`: high-income and emerging-economy descriptive comparison.
- `table7_econometric_results.csv`: pooled, fixed-effects, and random-effects log-log estimates with clustered inference.
- `table8_predictive_validation.csv`: expanding-window validation comparison across predictive models and benchmarks.
- `table9_model_selection_and_test.csv`: 2022–2023 benchmark comparison.
- `table10_scenario_sensitivity_summary.csv`: 2023 anchor and modeled 2028 scenario means.
- `sm_table_s7a_within_between.csv`: within-between elasticity decomposition.
- `sm_table_s7b_ppp_robustness.csv`: constant-PPP GDP robustness coefficients.
- `sm_table_s8a_mundlak.csv`: cluster-robust Mundlak test.
- `sm_table_s8b_pesaran_cd.csv`: Pesaran cross-sectional-dependence diagnostic.
- `sm_table_s8c_driscoll_kraay.csv`: Driscoll-Kraay sensitivity inference.
- `sm_table_s13_foldwise_validation.csv`: fold-wise validation RMSE for CatBoost and strong benchmarks.
- `sm_table_s19_feature_importance.csv`: global CatBoost SHAP importance.
- `sm_table_s20_sequential_conformal.csv`: CatBoost and persistence conformal coverage/width comparison.
- `sm_table_s21a_scenario_backtest.csv`: rolling-origin scenario backtests.
- `sm_table_s21b_predictor_support.csv`: historical and projected predictor support.
- `sm_table_s22_robustness_results.csv`: consolidated robustness summary.
- `sm_table_s23_independent_test.csv`: independent final-period comparison on the same 74 observations.
- `sm_table_s2_missing_data_summary.csv`: variable-level completeness and missingness.
- `sm_table_s3_robustness_strategy.csv`: robustness and sensitivity design.
